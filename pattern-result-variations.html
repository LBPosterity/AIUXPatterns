Result Variations

Overview

Providing variations for a single prompt input. AI's probabilistic nature means it can provide useful variations to a single prompt, allow users to view these variations to easily compare & contrast them.

User Archetype

Most users may find these extended results useful. Although, context is key. Does their workflow & use case require experimentation & creativity or does it call for more accurate, defined results. More is not always better.

Considerations

Not all users understand why an AI can deliver multiple responses to the same question. Consider if these extra results build or damage trust for your product. Do your users understand probabilities & why they can provide a range of useful results? Or could you be adding to the "black box" problem, making the mysterious AI more vague.
